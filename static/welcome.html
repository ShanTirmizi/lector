Lector is ready to read to you

Try it on this page. Press R, or use the button below.

The first time, the voice has to download (about 330 MB). After that it starts in a moment, and it works without a connection.

Where the name comes from

In the cigar factories of Cuba, rolling cigars by hand was quiet, repetitive work. From the 1860s the workers began paying one of their own to sit on a raised platform and read to them while they worked. That reader was called the lector.

Mornings were for newspapers. Afternoons were for novels, chosen by a vote of the workers, who would listen to a long book a chapter at a time over many weeks. A good lector did not just pronounce the words. They gave each character a voice, and kept a room of several hundred people following the story.

This extension does a smaller version of the same job. It reads the page in front of you, and marks each word as it goes so that your eyes can follow along or wander off.

Ways to start

  • R reads the page you are on. Press it again to pause.
  • Move the pointer over a paragraph and a small play button appears beside it.
  • Select some text and press Listen, or use Shift R.
  • Right-click anywhere in an article and choose "Read aloud from here".

While it reads

  • Drag along the yellow line at the bottom of the player to move through the text. It shows you where you will land.
  • Change the speed and the voice from the player. There are fifteen voices to try.
  • Scroll wherever you like. The page stops following you, and a button takes you back.
  • PDFs and Google Docs open in a clean reading view when you press play on them.

Yours alone

The voice runs on this computer. Nothing you read is sent anywhere, there is no account, and there is no limit on how much you listen.
